Platinum roadmap

Play it in this order. Story first, side stories next, then the long counts. Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable — it is flagged in red below and on its card.

  1. Step 1 — Story, Chapters 1–2. Play Gone Fishing, then Chapters 1 and 2. In Chapter 1, do Lucia’s balcony meditation (see Positive Mental Attitude) — it disappears when Chapter 1 ends. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Character switching unlocks after Breakfast Run.
  2. Step 2 — Story, Chapters 3–4. In Chapter 3, get the Snap Gun from Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs and do the time-sensitive Race Against Yago the moment it appears. Near the end of Chapter 4, do That’s Where The Money Is to unlock bank vaults.
  3. Step 3 — Finish the story. Complete the final mission for the gold trophy, and keep one untouched manual save from just before it. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus.
  4. Step 4 — Side-story finales. Finish the final missions for Andy (Lucia-only), Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone (Jason-only) and Bret’s hunt (Jason-only). All stay open after the credits, and 8 other stranger strands complete Strangers & Freaks.
  5. Step 5 — Robberies & partner trophies. 5 bank vaults, 1 armored truck, 1 jewelry store undetected, 1 illegal operation from each of the 9 gangs, 3 shops in a row with your partner, and a 4-star escape with your partner.
  6. Step 6 — Collectibles. 100 WAiNK scans, 3 card sets, 100 vehicles discovered, all 18 meditation spots, explore all of Leonida, 8 garages, 10 Scooter Bros registrations.
  7. Step 7 — Activities & the long grind. 250 stunts (300 for 100%), 42 shooting-range golds, 14 drone-race wins, 20 different race wins, 50 Challenges, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent — then finish the 100% checklist for A Long Road, and the Platinum pops.

⚠ Missable: Positive Mental Attitude — Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 is the only trophy launch-week guides flag as possibly missable. Everything else is reported to stay available after the story.
