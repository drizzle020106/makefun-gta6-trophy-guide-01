Roadmap to the platinum — 7 steps

  1. Step 1 — Chapters 1–2. Play the story. In Chapter 1, do Lucia's balcony meditation (the one possibly missable trophy) and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay. Beat Cal at fishing and finish an arcade game while you are here.
  2. Step 2 — Chapters 3–4. Keep playing the story. Do Yago's Race Against Yago the moment it appears in Chapter 3 (time-sensitive). In Chapter 3, take the Snap Gun jewelry job; near the end of Chapter 4, take That's Where The Money Is to open the bank vaults.
  3. Step 3 — Finish the story. Complete the final mission (gold). Keep an untouched manual save from just before it. After the credits, free roam stays open and the 100% checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion.
  4. Step 4 — Six side-story finales. Finish the final missions for Andy (Lucia-only), Billy, Kenton, Randy, Tyrone (Jason-only) and Bret's hunting questline (Jason-only). All stay open after the credits.
  5. Step 5 — Robberies & partner jobs. 5 bank vaults, a jewelry store unseen, an armored truck, one illegal operation per gang (9 gangs), 3 shops in a row with your partner, and a 4-star escape with your partner. These feed the money trophies too.
  6. Step 6 — Collectibles. WAiNK It at 100 locations, 3 card sets, 100 vehicles discovered, every meditation spot, and explore all of Leonida. Scan traffic with WAiNK as you travel — it counts for two trophies at once.
  7. Step 7 — The long grind + 100%. 250 stunts (300 for 100%), 50 Challenges (70 for 100%), 20 races, drone-race wins at all 14 locations, gold at all 42 Shooting Range challenges, then finish the 100% checklist for A Long Road (gold) — and the platinum pops right after.

Missable warning: only Positive Mental Attitude is reported as possibly missable (the Chapter 1 balcony meditation). Every other trophy stays available after the story. Never use cheats on your trophy save — they should switch off trophies and saving.
