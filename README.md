The best offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of
Grand Theft Auto VI: every trophy with its official name, description and
grade, a guide in our own words with its sources, a roadmap to the
platinum, missable flags, and a checklist whose progress is saved in your
browser.

## How to use

Open `app/index.html` in desktop Chrome — no network needed, everything
runs from the files in `app/`:

```sh
node serve.ts   # serves app/ at http://localhost:8080
```

- **Checklist**: tick a trophy when you earn it; progress is saved in
  localStorage and survives a refresh.
- **Search & filters**: find a trophy by name, or filter by kind (story,
  missable, collectibles, activities), grade, or earned / not earned.
- **Spoiler mode**: hides the names and descriptions of hidden story
  trophies until you earn them.
- **Roadmap**: the order to earn them in, from the official descriptions,
  with every missable trophy flagged.
- **Export / import**: save your progress to a file and load it back.

## The trophies

55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze — 28 of them hidden
on PlayStation. The list in `app/data/trophies.json` was verified against
Exophase, PSNProfiles, Planet GTA 6, Output Lag and Push Square; every
guide cites its source, and the few trophies whose unlock method is
unconfirmed are marked as such rather than invented.
